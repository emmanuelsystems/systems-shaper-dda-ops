## 5. Repeatable Post-Meeting-to-Execution Sequence

1. **Recover sources.** Freeze transcript/recording references, meeting analysis, coordination direction, governing repo evidence, and human permissions. Record missing sources instead of filling gaps.
2. **Interpret direction.** Separate confirmed direction, proposals, conflicts, and unknowns. Generate no more than three candidate goals.
3. **Select and freeze one goal.** Choose the highest-value bounded goal; name observable outcome, measures, permissions, success, failure, hold, and decision owner.
4. **Delegate bounded tasks.** Give each task a goal link, owner, output, dependency, allowed evidence, gate, prohibited actions, and escalation condition.
5. **Return evidence.** Specialists report artifacts, checks, burden, interventions, assumptions, defects, and proof limits without self-acceptance.
6. **Reconcile and freeze.** Root resolves conflicts explicitly, freezes an exact candidate manifest and SHA, and stops candidate movement.
7. **Verify independently.** Verifier reviews the frozen set against the goal and measurement contract and returns one scoped verdict without editing it.
8. **Measure and decide.** Root reports observed values versus thresholds; the human decides `accept`, `revise`, `hold`, or `reject` and separately authorizes any outward write or promotion.
9. **Iterate only from the decision.** A later loop may package a proven pattern into a workflow, skill, automation, or interface only under separate authorization and validation.

This sequence operationalizes the meeting's intent -> goal -> plan -> delegation -> evidence -> verification -> measurement -> decision chain while preserving the human gate (E2, E3, E6-E9).
