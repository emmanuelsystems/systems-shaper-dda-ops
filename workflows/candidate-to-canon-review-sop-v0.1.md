# Candidate to Canon Review SOP v0.1

Status: `candidate_review_only`

This SOP defines review states for workflow artifacts and templates.

## States

- `draft`: A working artifact created from available context.
- `candidate`: A draft that appears coherent enough for review.
- `review-ready`: A candidate with source anchors, proof boundary, and open questions stated.
- `accepted`: Human review has accepted the artifact for the stated scope.
- `canon`: Human review has approved the artifact as a durable source rule or reusable workflow.
- `runtime-ready`: The artifact has separate validation showing it works in the intended live runtime.

## Important Rule

Accepted does not mean canon.

Canon does not mean runtime-ready.

Runtime-ready requires separate validation.

## Promotion Rule

Promotion between states must name the reviewer, evidence, decision date, and proof boundary.
